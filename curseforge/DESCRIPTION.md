# GabbaGuildCraft

**Your recipes. Your guild. Ready to share.**

GabbaGuildCraft turns your learned WoW Classic profession recipes into shareable guild recipe lists. Built for Saftladen on Soulseeker.

- Scan learned recipes, reagents, quantities, item stats and effects.
- Search recipes and filter by relevance or equipment level.
- Copy Discord-sized messages with optional Wowhead links.
- Export HTML through SavedVariables for a guild crafting website.
- Open the recipe window from a movable minimap button.
- Save your export and reload the UI directly from the addon window.

No additional addon libraries required. The addon itself makes no network connections. Its controls are German; English recipe data is supported. The GabbaCraft website offers German and English.

## Quick start

Open your profession, clear its filters, then click the minimap icon or type `/ggc`. Click **Rezepte einlesen** to scan. Use **Text markieren** to copy text for Discord, or **HTML speichern** followed by **Neu laden** to prepare a website upload.

Your export is `WTF/Account/YOUR_ACCOUNT/SavedVariables/GabbaGuildCraft.lua`. Upload that file on [GabbaCraft](https://kellerkind.info/gabbacraft/) under My workshop. The Saftladen website requires its **Crafter** Discord role for uploads. Public recipe pages and the addon itself do not require that role.

## Deutsch

Lies deine gelernten Berufsrezepte mit Zutaten und Effekten ein und teile sie mit deiner Gilde. Das verschiebbare Hammer-Buch-Symbol an der Minimap öffnet das Fenster; alternativ funktioniert `/ggc` oder `/gts`.

**Beruf öffnen → Rezepte einlesen → HTML speichern → Neu laden → Exportdatei in GabbaCraft hochladen.** Für reine Discord-Listen reicht „Text markieren“ und Kopieren.
